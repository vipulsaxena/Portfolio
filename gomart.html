Context · Business

The bet we had to win

H1 2021 north star: 2M monthly transactions by June. Within that, Consumer Journey owned one critical reliability KR — visit-to-book conversion from 14% → 25%.

GoMart had to shift from ad-hoc efficiency shopping to routine competent grocery — planners who buy weekly essentials, not one-off emergency orders. Discovery was the leak; the Studio produced multiple bets to close it.

Solution 1 · Key · Design & Validate

Bottom navigation IA redesign

Navbar Needs Exploration · R&I · June 2021

Not an experiment — a proven pattern. GoFood had already shipped this exact move: −3.7% time-to-book, +23% clicks, 2× reorder-page traffic, and 70% of new GoMart shoppers already knew it.

Why a navbar, once hygiene is fixed

Poor search relevance was persistent across studies (Mar–Jun 2021) — fix content discovery first, then let the navbar be the silver bullet for everything else.

  • →Adds visible structure to overcrowded content
  • →Opens space to showcase existing and new features
  • →Simplifies the ineffective back-and-forth navigation in-app

→ That confidence became a dedicated navbar-needs study — three phased releases, one taken into testing next.

Solution 1 · Key · Three phased releases, one tested

From MVP to the concept we tested

I designed three progressively richer releases before this went to research — each one a hypothesis about what the navbar needed to solve. Only the most complete release went into the June 2021 concept-testing study with 6 IDIs.

Release 1 · MVP

Explore · Search · My Orders

Hypothesis: promoting order history to its own tab — with a one-tap "Order again" that rebuilds the cart — will lift reorder rate for routine shoppers.

Desk research had flagged low visibility for the reorder widget and past-purchase section.

Release 2 · +Merchant

Explore · Merchant · Search · My Orders

Hypothesis: giving Merchant its own destination — store grouping, branches, categories — will cut the back-and-forth between stores.

Targets the ~50% merchant-page drop-off flagged in desk research.

Release 3 · +Promo

Explore · Merchant · Search · Promo · My Orders

Hypothesis: consolidating deal content into one destination will lift engagement for deal-driven shoppers — addressing the "inefficient deal browsing" and low "Deals of the Day" visibility research flagged.

→ Taken into concept testing · 6 IDIs, June 2021

One hypothesis from this release didn't hold up: a dedicated Search tab. Testing disproved it — refinements on the next slide.

Solution 1 · Key · Design & Validate · Concept testing · June 2021 · 6 IDIs

What 6 IDIs told us about Release 3

We walked participants through the full 5-tab wireframe from the previous slide — Explore, Merchant, Search, Promo, and My Orders — and tagged expectations, delights, and pains per menu.

Navbar needs exploration — shopping modes & tab priorities

1 / 7

What held up

  • →Merchant — add branch selection, ratings, delivery fee upfront
  • →Promo — customers want membership & loyalty content, not just discounts
  • →Order — merge ongoing/past into one page instead of tabs

Explore kept its name (GoFood precedent); search bar + recs added to the homepage.

Before build · Discoverability

Coachmark/tutorial became mandatory — no participant recognised the bottom nav without prompting.

Search stayed on Explore, not as a tab — matching GoFood behaviour and what participants already expected from the homepage.

Solution 1 · Key · From research to visual design

The structure held in research — so I designed for it

Bottom navigation wasn't a leap of faith. GoFood had proven the pattern, 6 IDIs validated Release 3, and the same five-tab IA carried straight into visual design.

Visually: wireframe placeholders became GoMart's brand system — green accents, photography, real merchant storefronts, and product-led promo cards. Structure unchanged; fidelity upgraded.

Highlight · Solution 2 · Trust · Effective Communication

Order-tracking widget & chat

Trust · mid-order

A parallel Studio bet on trust after booking. During an active order, consumers chat with both shopper and driver — from the Gojek homepage track widget, Chat tab, or an in-flow modal. Born from service blueprint gaps: no customer–shopper chat, verbal consent only.

Highlight · Solution 3 · Discover · Maximized Value

Onboarding stories

Discover · first visit

The fastest bet to ship from the same brainstorm — full-screen story cards that explain value before a cluttered home: choose your store, search across stores, hand-picked by shoppers, instant delivery.

Full-screen story cards with animated entry and exit transitions for first-time GoMart users.
